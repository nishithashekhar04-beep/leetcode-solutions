## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used two loops to check every possible pair of elements in the array. If the sum of any two elements is equal to the target value, their indices are displayed as the answer.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The program was tested locally with different test cases. The test cases included a normal case and a case where the required pair was present at different positions in the array.
